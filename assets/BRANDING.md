# MELEK Branding

## Logo

The MELEK logo is the Kurdish flag in **horizontal orientation**:

```
+------------------------------------------+
|                  RED                     |
+------------------------------------------+
|           WHITE  ☀  (sun center)         |
+------------------------------------------+
|                  GREEN                   |
+------------------------------------------+
```

- Top stripe: Red
- Middle stripe: White with 21-ray golden sun centered
- Bottom stripe: Green

The flag is rotated from its traditional vertical-stripe layout to horizontal
so that it reads as a standard flag format, consistent with other red/white/green
flags (Iranian, Hungarian, etc.).

## Colors

| Element | Color |
|---|---|
| Red stripe | #D01C1F (Kurdish flag red) |
| White stripe | #FFFFFF |
| Green stripe | #007A3D (Kurdish flag green) |
| Sun | #F7C815 (Kurdish flag gold) |

## The Sun

21 rays. Centered on the white stripe. The 21 rays represent Newroz
(Kurdish New Year), which falls on the 21st of March.

## Usage

- The horizontal flag is the primary MELEK logo
- Use on Condenser frontend, witness pages, all chain branding
- The flag represents the Kurdish identity of the chain and the
  broader community MELEK serves
